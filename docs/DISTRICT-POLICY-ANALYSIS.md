# Mill Valley School District policy analysis for PTA yearbook photo collection

Reviewed 2026-09-11 from the Mill Valley School District policy library: <https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/PolicyListing.aspx?S=36030331>.

This is a policy-to-product analysis, not a legal opinion or a finding that the proposed app is approved. “Explicit” below means the district policy text says it. “Inference” means an application of that text to the proposed workflow. The policy pages identify their own reference tables as informational only; the analysis relies on the policy and regulation text, not on treating those reference tables as additional district policy.

## Product scope used for this review

The proposed first release is a public, upload-only page for families. It has no accounts and no public gallery. A parent uploads a photo or video with a typed name and optional note. A Cloudflare Worker sends it to a private Gumnut library, where PTA volunteers review and organize it, then export originals to Jostens Yearbook Avenue. A shared Gumnut login is proposed. The working assumption is a PTA project under `yearbook.oldmillpta.org`, with a temporary `workers.dev` address. Future import of school portraits pre-tagged with name, grade, or teacher is expressly outside this release and is not treated as approved here.

## Findings that directly affect the first release

### 1. PTA status and authority are a threshold question

**Explicit.** BP 1230, *School-Connected Organizations* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), expressly includes a “parent-teacher association or organization” as a school-connected organization. It says such an organization must be established and maintained as a separate entity from the school or district; its activities must follow law, Board policies, administrative regulations, and sponsoring-school rules; and proposed organizations must submit a request to the Board for authorization. [BP 1230](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=v0Abbq8RwRseplusgfHaFFDjg==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 1230, *School-Connected Organizations* (same date), requires an authorization request to state the organization’s name and purpose, bylaws/rules/procedures, officers, objectives, school principal signature, and insurance evidence when required. It also says the organization must not act as the district’s agent, must use a separate name/logo unless the Superintendent or designee consents, and is responsible for its own operations and records. [AR 1230](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=2T62bSgcplushR84EUzqvBAUw==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** The PTA should establish that it is an authorized school-connected organization and obtain the district/school rules or approval that govern the yearbook activity before representing the page as a district or school service. The separate-entity rule supports clear PTA branding and a clear statement about who operates the upload page. It does not, by itself, answer whether the district has authorized the PTA to collect, store, or export student-identifiable media.

**Unresolved.** The policy library does not establish whether this PTA/yearbook activity already has the required authorization, who the district designee is, whether the school has issued activity-specific rules, or whether the district considers the proposed collection to be a district function.

### 2. A parent upload does not replace district release permissions

**Explicit.** BP 5125.1, *Release of Directory Information* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), authorizes the Superintendent or designee to release directory information to news media or nonprofit organizations subject to policy and regulation, while allowing the district to limit or deny categories in students’ interests. It prohibits disclosure to private profit-making entities except for stated exceptions. [BP 5125.1](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=3JTIbOkWkEMzbD9TOfsiSg==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 5125.1, *Release of Directory Information* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), defines directory information as specified student-record information such as name, address, telephone/email, date of birth, participation in officially recognized activities, dates of attendance, degrees/awards, and prior school. It requires annual notice of the categories the district plans to release and the recipients, notice of the right to refuse designation/release, and a written parent/guardian opt out. It requires written consent before releasing directory information for a student identified as homeless, and says valid former-student opt outs continue. [AR 5125.1](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=vJTcV9KG8nUslshslshslsh3nJun71g==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** A private upload into a non-public PTA review library is materially different from publishing directory information on the district website, but the media and any names included in optional notes can still identify a child. The form asks for the uploader's name, not a student's name. The proposed upload disclaimer may document the family’s submission to the PTA; it does not demonstrate that the district made the annual notification required by AR 5125.1, that the PTA is a permitted recipient, or that a family’s existing written opt out has been checked. The app should not claim that a submission establishes permission for every pictured child.

The 5125.1 exhibit is an adopted PDF exhibit, last revised/reviewed 2022-06-16, but the live viewer exposed no readable pages during this review. A direct fetch of the embedded PDF returned an Incapsula request-block HTML page instead of a PDF; text extraction could not proceed. Do not infer the exhibit’s exact checkbox categories or wording from the exhibit link alone. [5125.1-E PDF exhibit](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=eEVtEVGS7DRGqyc2tqBTOw==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Unresolved.** The policies do not say that a printed yearbook is directory-information release, do not specify a yearbook/photo-release form, and do not say whether the district’s annual opt-out list is shared with the PTA. The policy listing search did not surface a standalone policy titled “photo release,” “picture release,” or “yearbook.” Obtain the actual district photo-release form/contract and its scope before treating the proposed disclaimer as sufficient. In particular, “yearbook” and “internet/public website” should be treated as separate uses until the district confirms otherwise.

### 3. Website publication rules matter if the PTA page or later sharing becomes public

**Explicit.** BP 1113, *District and School Websites* (last revised/reviewed 2024-05-22), says the Superintendent/designee must protect privacy on district and school websites. It bars publishing an individual student photograph accompanied by the student’s name or other PII without prior written parent/guardian consent. It permits photos of individual or groups of students when names are not included. [BP 1113](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=cOoU7cBOBgq6P3v9uzoPqA==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 1113, *District and School Websites* (last revised/reviewed 2024-05-22), requires accessibility features such as captions for videos and text alternatives to images, content guidelines and approval before posting, editorial review, and security procedures to prevent unauthorized access and changes. It separately requires written permission from the student and parent/guardian for student work published on district or school websites. [AR 1113](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=slshPPvfg7omQ2gwSH8dlBCLw==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

E 1113(1), revised/reviewed 2022-10-13, lists materials the district must prominently post online, including LCAP, agendas, and required notices. It does not supply a photo-release or yearbook consent form. [E 1113(1)](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=bplusGrJmJf0U8JEme0VJLj1Q==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** The current upload-only page has no gallery, so these website-publication rules do not automatically establish that an upload itself is a prohibited district-web publication. If the page is held out as a district/school website, or if volunteers later post images, names, videos, or links publicly, the written-consent, review, accessibility, and security requirements become directly relevant. The safest first-release contract is private intake plus private review, with an explicit prohibition on public posting from the app.

The policy text does not say that a PTA-owned domain is a “district or school website.” That classification depends on ownership, branding, authorization, and how the district presents the page.

### 4. Gumnut, Cloudflare, and Jostens create a third-party storage/processing question

**Explicit.** BP 5125, *Student Records* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), requires regulations governing identification, collection, retention, and security of student records, and training for personnel handling sensitive student information. It says the Superintendent/designee may contract with a third party for digital storage, management, and retrieval of student records, or authorize a digital-software provider to access, store, and use them, only if the contract meets Education Code 49073.1 and other applicable state and federal law. [BP 5125](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=ssDREH7WMoPp8Vs19s2lHQ==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 5125, *Student Records* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), defines a student record broadly as information in any medium directly related to an identifiable student and maintained by the district, required in an employee’s duties, or maintained by a party acting for the district. It defines PII to include the student’s name and information that can reasonably identify the student. It defines a contractor/consultant as a person with a formal written district agreement for outsourced services and expressly says a volunteer or other party is not a contractor. Access is limited to authorized persons and legitimate educational interest; recipients generally may not re-release records without written permission. [AR 5125](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=CEwEoLlPF7omVwELItzdRw==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** If the district treats the PTA workflow as collecting or maintaining records for the district, the Worker, Gumnut, and Jostens handoffs need a district-authorized contractual/privacy path; a shared Gumnut login is not evidence of that path and weakens individual access attribution. If the PTA is operating independently as the separate entity described in BP/AR 1230, the record classification and contractual trigger may differ, but the policies do not decide that status. As product recommendations, document the data path, limit library access to authorized reviewers, establish permitted vendor uses, and provide a removal-request route. These recommendations are not a finding that the policies expressly require the same contract for every independent PTA activity.

**Unresolved.** No policy page reviewed supplies the required Education Code 49073.1 contract terms, identifies an existing district agreement with Gumnut, Cloudflare, or Jostens, says where data is hosted, describes subprocessors, or promises a deletion SLA. Do not represent private Gumnut storage as district-approved storage until those facts are confirmed.

### 5. Volunteer access is regulated, but the policy does not answer the shared-login design

**Explicit.** BP 1240, *Volunteer Assistance* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), requires a district volunteer plan for recruiting, screening, and placing volunteers; appropriate orientation/training; and compliance with district policies, regulations, and school rules. [BP 1240](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=5IqCIKAp1yqtMMGVrpo09g==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 1240, *Volunteer Assistance* (same date), permits volunteers to perform other duties supporting district/school operations when approved by the Superintendent/designee. It requires fingerprint clearance before assuming a volunteer position working with students in a district-sponsored student activity program, while stating that the Superintendent/designee determines which positions are covered. It also provides exceptions for certain supervised nonteaching roles and requires TB assessment/examination on initial assignment unless exempted for limited contact. [AR 1240](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=N4plus7qiNiagjqRsAb4floZg==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=zxfvZYmcKkpluslhKHzD4ftFA==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** Volunteers who access identifiable student media for a school-connected yearbook should be named, approved, trained, and assigned least-privilege access. The policy supports asking the Superintendent/designee whether yearbook reviewers are a covered student-activity position; it does not authorize the app team to decide that fingerprint/TB requirements do or do not apply. A shared login also prevents reliable per-person audit and removal, so it is a poor fit for the policy’s authorization and security concepts even though the policy does not expressly ban shared credentials.

### 6. Retention and removal are not solved by “private” status

**Explicit.** AR 3580, *District Records* (adopted/revised/reviewed 2022-06-16), classifies district records as permanent, optional, or disposable and says records of continuing usefulness remain unclassified until that usefulness ceases. It also says electronically stored information related to district business must be saved to a district-provided account/device and retained under the classification rules. [AR 3580](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=Mx3A2Obtoag6ukKOyljcSQ==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=y1ZW0qRGjEafuplusqEjNeK2Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

AR 5125 separately lists logs of persons/organizations requesting or receiving student-record information and parent restrictions on directory information among mandatory interim records, subject to destruction after usefulness ceases under the stated rule. [AR 5125](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=CEwEoLlPF7omVwELItzdRw==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=9slshUHzTHxaaYMVf6zKpJz3Q==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** A PTA-operated library may have its own retention policy under BP/AR 1230, while a district-maintained collection may trigger district classification and retention rules. Determine the applicable retention schedule and removal obligations before promising a deletion date. If student-record rules apply, evaluate their access-log requirements and exceptions. The proposed Worker streams uploads without storing photo objects; vendor copies, exports, and operational logs need separate consideration. “No public gallery” reduces exposure but does not establish a retention period or right to deletion.

**Unresolved.** The policies do not state how long yearbook submissions, rejected media, backups, thumbnails, Worker logs, or Jostens exports must be retained, nor who can order removal after export. They also do not resolve whether submitted family photos become district records or remain PTA records.

### 7. AI policy is a future-processing constraint, not approval for portrait import

**Explicit.** BP 0441, *Artificial Intelligence*, adopted and reviewed 2026-06-04, calls for ethical and transparent AI use, accountability by users, and prioritizing security and privacy when changing practices or adopting AI. It directs consistency with the district technology, employee technology, student technology, and student-record policies. [BP 0441](https://simbli.eboardsolutions.com/Policy/ViewPolicy.aspx?S=36030331&revid=9LnwSzIUxXpdslsh84tqiMXkQ==&ptid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&secid=amIgTZiB9plushNjl6WXhfiOQ==&PG=6&IRP=0&isPndg=false)

**Inference.** The policy is relevant if Gumnut, a Worker, Jostens, or a future service performs face recognition, tagging, transcription, ranking, generation, or other AI processing. Its privacy and accountability principles are relevant to Gumnut's proposed face grouping and other automated processing now, as well as to future imports. The text reviewed does not establish a blanket no-AI default or specific approval requirement for this PTA app; confirm applicable rules and permitted processing without silently changing the proposed architecture. It does not approve importing school portraits pre-tagged with name/grade/teacher, and this analysis makes no feature decision for that future idea.

## What this analysis resolves and what it cannot establish

The policy review is complete for the readable sources listed above; unreadable exhibits and the actual family release form remain evidence gaps. The upload-only design avoids publishing submitted photos on the collection page. It does not itself establish publication permission, district approval of storage, or a retention period.

The remaining facts to confirm are the PTA/yearbook activity's existing authority and record classification; the actual family photo/yearbook release and applicable opt outs; any applicable vendor agreements and authorized processing; and retention/removal obligations across the library and exported copies. Existing approvals may already resolve some of these points. This is not a finding that the PTA needs to repeat its organizational authorization or obtain every conceivable approval before implementation.

Communication and lead handoff remain Kari's responsibility outside the plan. Student-identification/tagging procedures and PTA domain setup remain outside the plan as directed. Policy implications for student data are documented here without assigning those operational tasks or approving a portrait-import feature.

The proposed disclaimer can be retained as a plain-language notice of the PTA’s intended yearbook use and private review, but it should not say or imply that it replaces the district’s annual directory-information notice, a written opt out, or a specific photo/yearbook release unless the district supplies that exact authorization.
